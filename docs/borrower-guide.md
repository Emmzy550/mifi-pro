## Before You Apply

### Who Can Apply?

You can apply for a loan if you:
- ✅ Are 18 years or older
- ✅ Have a regular source of income
- ✅ Have a valid phone number
- ✅ Live in the service area

### What You'll Need

Gather this information before you start:
- 📱 Your phone number and email
- 💼 Employment details (what type of work you do)
- 💰 Monthly income amount
- 📊 Monthly expenses (rent, food, transport, etc.)
- 💳 Any existing loans or debts
- 🎯 How much you want to borrow and why

**Optional but helpful:**
- Bank statements (last 3 months)
- Mobile money transaction history
- Utility bill payment records

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Visit the Application Portal

Go to the loan application website provided by your lender:
```
https://your-lender.com/apply
```

### Step 2: Fill in Your Information

**Personal Details**
- Your full name (as it appears on your ID)
- Phone number
- Email address (if you have one)

**Employment Information**
- Select your employment type:
  - **Salaried** - You receive a regular salary
  - **Trader** - You run a business or trade
  - **Farmer** - You earn from farming
  - **Self-Employed** - You work for yourself

**Financial Information**
- **Monthly Income**: How much you earn per month
- **Monthly Expenses**: How much you spend per month (rent, food, transport, school fees, etc.)
- **Existing Debt**: Any loans you're already paying

**Loan Request**
- **Amount**: How much you want to borrow
- **Purpose**: What you'll use the money for (be specific!)
  - ✅ Good: "Buy stock for my shop"
  - ✅ Good: "Pay school fees for 2 children"
  - ❌ Bad: "Personal use"

### Step 3: Submit Your Application

Click **"Submit Application"**

The system will:
1. Review your information (takes 2-3 seconds)
2. Calculate your risk score
3. Give you an instant preliminary result

## Understanding Your Results

After you submit, you'll see one of these results:

### ✅ Approved

**What it means:**
- You qualify for the loan!
- Your income is sufficient
- Your debt level is manageable

**What's next:**
- A loan officer will contact you within 1-2 business days
- They may ask for additional documents
- Once verified, money is disbursed to your account

**Interest Rate:**
- Typically 15-20% per year
- Depends on your risk profile

---

### ⚠️ Conditional Approval

**What it means:**
- You qualify, but with conditions
- Might be a **higher interest rate** (to reflect higher risk)
- Might be a **lower amount** than you requested

**Why this happens:**
- Your expenses are close to your income
- You have some existing debt
- Your income is irregular

**What's next:**
- Review the offered terms
- Decide if you can afford the higher rate
- Contact the lender if you have questions

---

### ❌ Not Approved

**What it means:**
- The system couldn't approve your loan at this time

**Common reasons:**
- Income is too low for the requested amount
- Too much existing debt
- Expenses are too high compared to income

**What you can do:**
1. **Apply for a smaller amount**
2. **Increase your income** (add another income source)
3. **Pay down existing debt** first
4. **Reduce expenses** where possible
5. **Try again in 3-6 months** after improving your finances

**Important:** This is not a rejection of you as a person - it's about financial math!

## Uploading Bank Statements (Optional)

Uploading your bank or mobile money statements can **improve your approval chances**!

### Why Upload Statements?

The system can see:
- ✅ Your income is regular and consistent
- ✅ You save money
- ✅ You pay bills on time
- ✅ Your spending is stable

This can help you get:
- **Lower interest rates**
- **Higher loan amounts**
- **Faster approvals**

### How to Upload

**Step 1:** Prepare your statements
- Bank statements (last 3 months)
- Mobile money statements (M-Pesa, Airtel Money, etc.)
- PDF, CSV, or Excel format

**Step 2:** Upload during application
- Look for "Upload Statements (Optional)"
- Click "Choose File"
- Select your statement file
- Click "Upload"

**Step 3:** Wait for analysis
- The system analyzes your transactions (10-15 seconds)
- You'll see your updated result

### Privacy & Security

🔒 **Your data is safe:**
- Statements are encrypted
- Only used for your loan assessment
- Not shared with anyone else
- Automatically deleted after 30 days

## What Happens Next

### Timeline

**Day 1: Application Submitted**
- ✅ You get instant preliminary result
- ✅ Application sent to loan officer

**Day 1-2: Review**
- 📞 Loan officer may call you
- 📄 May ask for additional documents (ID, pay slips, etc.)

**Day 2-3: Final Decision**
- ✅ Final approval or rejection
- 📧 You receive notification (SMS/Email)

**Day 3-5: Disbursement** (if approved)
- 💰 Money sent to your M-Pesa or bank account
- 📱 You receive confirmation
- 📋 Repayment schedule sent to you

### After Approval

**Read Your Loan Agreement:**
- Loan amount
- Interest rate
- Repayment schedule (when payments are due)
- Total amount to repay
- Late payment penalties

**Set Up Reminders:**
- Mark payment dates on your calendar
- Set phone reminders
- Consider automatic payments if available

**Keep Communications:**
- Save all SMS messages
- Keep loan agreement safe
- Note your loan reference number

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does it take to get a result?**
- Initial assessment: 2-3 seconds
- Final decision: 1-3 business days

**Can I apply again if rejected?**
- Yes! Wait at least 1 month before reapplying
- Work on improving your financial situation first

**What if I made a mistake in my application?**
- Contact the lender immediately
- Provide the correct information
- Don't submit multiple applications

**Is my information secure?**
- Yes, all data is encrypted and protected
- Only used for loan processing
- Not sold or shared with third parties

**Do I need a computer?**
- No! You can apply from your phone
- Works on any browser
- Simple and easy to use

**What if I can't pay on time?** 
- Contact your lender IMMEDIATELY
- Don't hide - they can help!
- Options may include:
  - Short extension
  - Modified payment plan
  - Reduced amount for this month

**Can I pay early?**
- Usually yes!
- May save on interest
- Check with your lender about early payment terms

## Tips for a Successful Application

💡 **Be Honest**
Provide accurate information. Lying can lead to rejection and future difficulties.

💡 **Choose the Right Amount**
Only borrow what you truly need and can afford to repay.

💡 **Have a Clear Purpose**
Know exactly what you'll use the money for. "Business expansion" is better than "general use."

💡 **Upload Statements**
If you have good transaction history, upload it! It can help a lot.

💡 **Keep Your Phone On**
The loan officer may need to call you. Answer all calls!

💡 **Be Patient**
Quality assessment takes time. Don't apply to multiple lenders simultaneously.

💡 **Plan for Repayment**
Before applying, make sure you can afford the monthly payments.
